# Channel matching

Channel numbers, display names, and IDs presented to Plex stay independent of upstream sources. Matching normalises case, Unicode/punctuation, country labels, quality suffixes (including attached UHD), and known brand variants. It compares complete service names rather than unrestricted fuzzy substrings, so CNN International, MTV2, and Discovery spin-offs remain distinct.

Other provider names are additional positive aliases. Prefix a word or phrase with `-` (optional following space) to exclude it; exclusions are case-insensitive and apply to source names and provider TVG IDs. Country boundaries are respected, including explicit dotted guide-ID suffixes such as `.in` (India). In display names, ordinary words such as “in” and “and” remain part of the name. Compact guide IDs also honour exclusions when they have a recognised channel boundary: `CinemaxPacific.us` is excluded by `-pacific`, while `STARZEncoreWesterns.us` is not excluded by `-west`. There is no hard-coded US East/West preference; use channel exclusions such as `-west` where wanted. Learned TVG identities may provide alternate names but cannot override explicit exclusions. Recognised guide IDs are checked even before they have been learned. If the name and guide ID identify different services (for example NBC and `cnbc.us`), the feed stays unassigned. Bundled service names also supply conflict checks when that service is absent from the selected lineup. Opaque numeric or unfamiliar IDs are not treated as proof of a different service. An ID learned for several channels is not reused automatically.

Ambiguous matches are left unassigned and are not automatically imported from provider catalogues. Manual enabled/disabled assignments persist through rematching, except that exclusions, required-station rules, and recognised identity conflicts remove forbidden enabled assignments. New streams and changed metadata are rematched incrementally; edits to channel rules re-evaluate affected streams against all selected channels so ambiguities are handled consistently. A matching-engine update re-evaluates saved streams once without changing channel definitions or verification history. Changing a channel number does not rematch feeds.

Provider `VIP` and `[BK]` decorations are ignored for name identity; the UHD eligibility setting still applies independently. Catalogue headings such as `##### TENNIS #####` are not treated as television feeds.

Fresh installations include 150 editable US channels. Tests and preset fixtures live beside the catalogue code; the runtime preset is authoritative. Existing installations retain their own channels.

The US preset includes CBS (002/WCBS), NBC (004/WNBC), FOX (005/WNYW), ABC (007/WABC), MyNetworkTV (009/WWOR), The CW (011/WPIX), and PBS (013/WNET). Each requires its New York station ID. A generic network or “New York” label alone is insufficient. A name identifying WNBC with a guide ID identifying KNBC is rejected. For example `US NBC HD` with guide ID `nbcwnbc.us` qualifies, but the same name with no ID, `cnbc.us`, or a different affiliate does not. Related news, sports, children's services, and explicitly labelled western feeds are excluded.

**Output → Add or edit a channel → Required station ID** is optional and works with any lineup. It requires the station in the source name or guide ID. Names use complete tokens; guide IDs also recognise a combined network/station label. Exclusions and country rules still apply. Primary and standby feeds come from the same constrained roster. Tightening a rule removes incompatible memberships without adding failure strikes; accounts are retained. The field is included in channel exports and manual full backups, using schema-8 metadata.

Provider labels can be false. These checks do not inspect programme content or recognise holding screens. Verify the picture and local guide mapping before relying on a channel for recordings. A strict roster may be empty when none of the available feeds identifies the required station.

Updating Antennarr does not add these channels to a saved lineup. Add missing channels individually under Output, or deliberately use **Bulk operations → Reset to default US channels** to replace the entire lineup and rebuild its rosters. That reset removes custom channels and rules.
